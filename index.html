How long it lasts

Gel, week by week.
No surprises.

The question everyone asks and almost nobody answers straight. New growth does not chip the polish — it pushes it away from the cuticle.

  1. Day 1

    Full gloss

    Cured hard and flush to the cuticle. Shower, cook and type within the hour.

  2. Week 2

    First regrowth

    A millimetre shows at the base. Still even, still glossy — nothing needs doing.

  3. Week 3

    Visible line

    Reads at arm's length. Most clients rebook here, before the free edge lifts.

  4. Week 4

    Time to rebook

    The overlay starts to lever on new growth. Come in for a proper soak-off.
