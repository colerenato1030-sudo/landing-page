This season

Four finishes,
one shape.

Every finish here sits on the same almond shape and the same hand-filed prep. Choose the surface — the structure underneath does not change.

  • Pressed Blossom

    Sheer nude with a floral inlay and a scatter of gold fleck. Our only art finish that still reads as bare.

  • Mauve Glaze

    Deep taupe with a mauve cast and a full gloss top. The most forgiving of the four as it grows out.

  • Milk Rose

    Opaque milky pink, built in thin coats so the free edge stays translucent rather than chalky.

  • Bare Blush

    The palest wash we mix, and the one the studio is named for. Barely there, and deliberately so.

How long it lasts

Gel, week by week.
No surprises.

The question everyone asks and almost nobody answers straight. This is what a Barely Gloss set actually looks like as it grows out.

Day 1

Full gloss

Cured hard and flush to the cuticle. Safe to shower, cook and type within the hour.

Week 2

First regrowth

About a millimetre shows at the base. Still even, still glossy — nothing needs doing yet.

Week 3

Visible line

Regrowth reads at arm's length. Most clients rebook here, before the free edge starts to lift.

Week 4

Time to rebook

Past here the overlay levers on new growth and can take natural layers with it. Come in for a proper soak-off.
